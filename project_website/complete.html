Project Overview

Personal AI Wardrobe Assistant is an AI-powered platform that combines outfit recommendation, virtual try-on, and wardrobe management into one seamless experience.

It is designed to make everyday dressing simpler and more intuitive, helping users discover personalized styles, make confident decisions, and get more value from their wardrobe.

Recommendation AI

Generate personalized outfit suggestions based on your wardrobe, preferences, weather, and occasion, with clear explanations for each recommendation.

Virtual Try-On

Upload your photo and preview how different clothes or full outfits look on you through realistic AI-generated images.

My Wardrobe

Manage your clothing items with easy upload, automatic tagging, search, and filtering for efficient organization.

My Calendar

Plan and record daily outfits using a calendar view, and directly add recommended looks into your schedule.

Wardrobe Analysis

Analyze your wardrobe usage with visual insights and receive suggestions to improve your clothing collection.
